The Parisian DJ, producer and remixer has worked with and remixed artists including David Guetta, Jean-Michel Jarre, Gloria Estefan, Dead or Alive, Sophie Ellis-Bextor, Hatiras, Robbie Rivera and SASH!, while his music has received support from house artists including Pete Tong, Erick Morillo, Danny Rampling and Boris Dlugosch.
From residencies at Paris clubs Queen and Palace to international stages and records including “Gold,” “Live Your Dreams” and “Give Some Love,” Clamaran has been part of the global house music scene since the early days. His productions range from filtered disco to driving tribal beats.

HOUSE OF OM 20TH ANNIVERSARY: COLETTE B2B DJ HEATHER
The 20th anniversary of the iconic House of OM: Colette & DJ Heather compilation marks a milestone for two pioneers of the electronic music scene. Released in June 2006 on OM Records, this double-disc mix became a definitive moment for Chicago house music, spotlighting the lifelong friendship and musical synergy of two of the genre’s most formidable talents.
Before they became global headliners, Colette and DJ Heather forged their bond in the fertile underground of 1990s Chicago, driven by a shared love for the city’s signature four-on-the-floor sound. By the mid-2000s, both artists had been signed to San Francisco’s powerhouse label, OM Records, establishing themselves as heavy hitters who performed hundreds of shows annually. This culminated in the release of House of OM: Colette & DJ Heather. The double-album perfectly captured their complementary sonic identities:
Disc 1 (Colette) sparkled with smooth, funky, vocal-heavy tracks, featuring her own performance on “All The Same” alongside contributions from Kaskade, Andy Caldwell, and Greenskeepers.
Disc 2 (DJ Heather) delivered a deeper, instrumental-focused exploration of Chicago jack, featuring tracks from Inland Knights, Giom, and Mark Farina.
The release was an instant underground and commercial favourite within the dance community, serving as a masterclass in track selection and seamless mixing. Following the release, the duo embarked on extensive North American tours with fellow OM label mates. The success of those shows laid the groundwork for their annual Second City Sessions tours, launched in 2009. This collaborative banner featured Colette and DJ Heather playing extended back-to-back sets, a tradition that has earned a massive, dedicated following over the decades.
Reflecting on their long personal friendship and the 20-year legacy of the House of OM era, both artists have deep gratitude for their enduring partnership and appreciation for the compilation that changed the trajectory of their careers. To mark the anniversary alongside the OM Records 30th Anniversary celebrations, we’re excited to bring them back to Toronto at Wiggle Room for a HOUSE OF OM 20-year extended set
